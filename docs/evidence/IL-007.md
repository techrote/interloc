## Security review
Sanitization happens before export-byte construction. Potential secret prefixes remain buffered until they can be proven not to form a configured literal, preventing boundary leakage. Control sequences are statefully removed across fragments. Image descriptors cannot claim remote delivery. Limits apply to bytes, not merely lines/characters. This layer deliberately documents that configured exact redaction is not a guarantee of finding every possible secret.
